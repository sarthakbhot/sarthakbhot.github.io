Project

Folio

A personal expense tracker and budget copilot. It watches the month with you: what already landed, what is still coming, and what the budget should do about it.

Next.js Cloudflare Workers vinext Plaid Web push

I built Folio because a list of transactions is not a budget. I wanted one place that could pull spending in, sort it, notice the charges that repeat, and tell me whether the month was still on track.

What it does

  • Card sync

    Transactions come in through Plaid, so the ledger is the cards, not a spreadsheet I have to update by hand.

  • Categories

    Spending is sorted into categories. Folio can remember a merchant, and the next charge from that place lands in the same bucket.

  • Recurring charges

    Subscriptions and other repeating charges are pulled out on their own, including what is still committed for the rest of the month.

  • Income

    An income view sits next to spending, so the month is money in and money out, not only a list of purchases.

  • Adaptive budgets

    Each category has a limit and a pace: on track, behind, or ahead. Unused room can roll forward. Alerts and web push, including from a home-screen install on iPhone, flag what needs a look.

  • Payoff planner

    Balances line up in avalanche order, so the expensive debt is the one the plan pays first.

  • Pay and shifts

    Log a shift, or a weekly pattern, and Folio estimates the paycheque, including tax, CPP, and EI. The figures in the screenshots are examples, not a filing.

  • Net worth

    Savings and investment accounts roll into one view of what is held, next to the spending.

  • Budget copilot

    A short setup asks about pay, a savings goal, and priorities. It starts from income, balances, recurring charges, and category averages it can already see, then writes a budget you can apply. The plan updates as new transactions land, with a check-in partway through the month.

How it's built

Folio is a Next.js app, deployed to Cloudflare Workers with vinext. Plaid links the accounts. The interface is dark, and it is laid out for a phone as well as a wide window, with a bottom tab bar on small screens. Alerts use web push. The screenshots on this page use fake demo data.
