Project

Folio

A personal expense tracker and budget copilot. It watches the month with you: what already landed, what is still coming, and what the budget should do about it.

Next.js vinext Cloudflare Workers D1 Plaid PWA Passkeys

Screenshots use fake demo data.

Overview.

The main screen. Spend so far this month, the pace against last month, each card's share, and how the categories split.

Activity.

Every charge in one list. Search it, filter by category and date, and export a CSV. Pending items stay marked.

Budgets.

A cap for each category, spent against that cap, and whether it is on track or over. Unused budget can roll forward.

Budget copilot.

It confirms the pay, cards, and recurring charges already in the ledger, then fills in the gaps.

Recurring.

Subscriptions and other repeating charges, detected from the ledger, with edit and remove.

Cards.

Issuers, balances, and the last four digits. A quiet card gets a nudge. New cards can be added from Settings.

Income.

Payroll grouped from the deposit name, cash-account balances beside it, and an optional pay rate.

Settings.

Connect a bank or card through Plaid. Consent comes first. Sign-in, export, and an access log sit on the same screen.

Case study

Problem
A list of transactions is not a budget. I wanted one place that could pull spending in, sort it, notice repeating charges, and show whether the month was still on track.
What I built
Overview for the month, activity with search and a CSV export, income grouped from deposit names, recurring charges, and category budgets. Budget copilot confirms pay, cards, and recurring charges, then fills in the gaps. Cards are grouped by issuer. Settings covers appearance, card names, Plaid connect, export, and an access log. Net worth, payoff, alerts, and pay (a cheque or hours) are in the app as well.
Stack
Next.js, deployed to Cloudflare Workers with vinext. D1 for the database. Plaid for linking a bank. A PWA, with passkeys through WebAuthn. Dark by default, with a Dark, Light, or System toggle.
Outcome
Folio is built and shown on this page with fake demo data. It is not linked as a public app.

How it's built

Folio is a Next.js app, deployed to Cloudflare Workers with vinext, and D1 holds the data. Plaid links a bank from Settings, after consent. It installs as a PWA, and a passkey can sign in through WebAuthn. The interface is dark by default. Settings offers Dark, Light, or System. The same screens work on a phone and in a wide window.
