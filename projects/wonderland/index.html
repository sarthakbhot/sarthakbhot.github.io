Wonderland

A console text adventure in Java. Six places, hidden treasures, and the Queen of Hearts. Pure Java, no dependencies.

JavaOOPFile I/O
Problem
Build a console text adventure in pure Java: a world you can walk, people you can talk to, and items you can carry, loaded from plain text files, with no dependencies.
What I built
Six connected locations, hidden treasures, and NPCs including the Mad Hatter and the Queen of Hearts. An inventory and a stash. The whole world loads from plain text files.
Stack
Java. Object-oriented design and file I/O. No libraries.
Outcome
Completed in April 2026. The source is on GitHub.
