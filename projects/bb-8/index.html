BB-8

A life-size BB-8 with a Raspberry Pi for a brain. Currently in the design phase.

Raspberry PiPythonAI3D printing
Problem
A life-size BB-8 that can recognize a face, greet someone by name, and respond. It is still in the design phase.
What I built
Nothing is assembled yet. I am 3D modeling the parts. The plan is a Pi camera for face recognition, a speaker so it can greet people by name, and AI-driven interaction.
Stack
Raspberry Pi, Python, and 3D printing. AI for recognition and conversation, once the body exists.
Outcome
Still in design. A build log is not written yet.
