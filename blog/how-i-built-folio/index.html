How I built Folio

An expense tracker and budget copilot. This note is the product and the stack, nothing else.

A list of transactions is not a budget. I wanted one place that could pull spending in, sort it, notice the charges that repeat, and tell me whether the month was still on track.

What it does

  • Overview shows month-to-date spend, pace against last month, each card's share, and a category split, with merchant and card filters.
  • Activity is a searchable list with category and date filters, pending charges, and a CSV export.
  • Income groups payroll from the deposit name, shows cash-account balances, and can store a pay rate.
  • Recurring lists subscriptions and other repeating charges. Each one can be edited or removed.
  • Budgets are envelopes per category: spent against the cap, on track or over, with unused budget rolled forward.
  • Budget copilot confirms pay, cards, and recurring charges, then fills in the gaps.
  • Cards and accounts are grouped by issuer, with balances, the last four digits, and a reminder when a card has gone quiet.
  • Settings covers appearance, renaming a card, Plaid connect and consent, data export, legal drafts, and an access log.
  • Net worth, payoff, alerts, and pay (a cheque or hours) are in the app as well.

Stack

Folio is a Next.js app, deployed to Cloudflare Workers with vinext. D1 holds the data. Plaid links a bank from Settings. Folio is a PWA, and sign-in can use a passkey through WebAuthn. The interface is dark by default, with a Dark, Light, or System toggle, and the layout works on a phone and in a wide window.

The screenshots on the project page use fake demo data. There is no public app link on this site.
