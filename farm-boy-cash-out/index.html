Loans come in whole $5 bills and full coin rolls of whatever you pick — never loose change from the office. So a small shortfall rounds up to the next whole bill or roll, and the difference is dropped back from the cash you counted. That’s how $198.80 takes a $5 bill and drops $3.80 back to land exactly on $200.00.
